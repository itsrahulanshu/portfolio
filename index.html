Building Products, Not Just Projects

My development philosophy focuses on creating real solutions for real problems

01

Identify Real Problems

I don't build for the sake of building. Every project starts with a genuine frustration or need I've experienced personally. The LPU timetable app came from waiting 30 seconds every time I needed my schedule. The MCQ generator was born from expensive practice test platforms. Real problems create real motivation.

02

Research Existing Solutions

Before writing a single line of code, I study what's already out there. What works? What doesn't? Where are the gaps? For the temporary email service, I tested 10+ existing platforms to understand their limitations before building something better. Good research prevents wasted effort.

03

Build Fast, Iterate Faster

Perfect is the enemy of done. I launch MVPs quickly to get real user feedback, then iterate based on actual usage patterns. The MCQ generator started with basic functionality and evolved based on student requests. Speed to market beats perfection in planning.

04

Focus on Performance

Users don't care about your tech stack—they care about speed and reliability. Every optimization decision is measured against user experience. The timetable app's 2-second load time isn't accidental; it's the result of deliberate performance-first architecture choices.

05

Make It Sustainable

Building is just the beginning. I design for maintainability, scalability, and long-term viability. Clean code, proper documentation, and sustainable monetization (like AdSense integration) ensure projects survive beyond the initial excitement. Sustainability enables impact.
